FAQ

Questions, answered

How much can be cleaned in 30 minutes?

Roughly one bathroom, a 3-seat sofa, or one room's dusting and floor. Choose 45 or 60 minutes for bigger or dirtier areas – the booking screen shows what each item covers.

Do I need to provide cleaning material?

No. The cleaner brings the vacuum, scrubbers, mops and eco-safe chemicals. Just keep water and electricity available.

Can I book more than one area?

Yes – book the festive combo (kitchen + bathroom), or book one item and add more in the notes; we'll confirm the final price on WhatsApp before the visit.

When do I pay?

After the work is done and you've checked it, by UPI or cash.

What if I need full-home deep cleaning?

Choose “Something else” in the booking flow and describe the job. A supervisor checks the area and shares the final price before starting.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes – reply on WhatsApp or call us before the slot and we'll move or cancel it free of charge.
