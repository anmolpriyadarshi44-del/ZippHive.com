FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there a visiting or inspection charge?

A small inspection charge may apply if you choose not to go ahead with the repair. If you approve the repair, it's adjusted in your bill. We always tell you upfront.

How fast can a technician reach me?

For mobile repair we aim to finish at your doorstep in about 60 minutes. For home services we usually schedule the same day or the next day, at a time you choose.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes. Every repair comes with a service warranty on parts and labour. The exact period depends on the job and is mentioned on your bill. Something wrong after a repair? Raise a warranty claim.

How do I pay?

Pay after the work is done — UPI, card or cash.

Is my phone data safe?

Mobile repairs happen in front of you at your doorstep, so your phone never leaves your sight.
